Stage automations

A named rule that runs when a deal enters a stage. Leave service and offer on “any” to match every deal, or narrow it. A task title and brief can use {partner_name}, {deal_id}, {stage}, {service_name}, {service_slug}, and {automation_name}. Child deals stay on the same partner, link back with parent_deal_id, and are stamped source=automation:<this automation’s id> with no offer and no price. Moving the deal into the stage again does not open a second task or child while the first is still open. Nothing here is built in: a new database starts empty, and creating a deal already in the stage does not fire.
