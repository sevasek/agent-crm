ICP fit criteria

ICP fit criteria are the rules this CRM uses to score how well a lead or deal matches the customer you want. Learn more ↗

A weighted criterion that contributes to a lead's fit score. For a tag, choose Company has tag or Lead or deal has tag, operator has_tag, and the tag slug (for example google-ads or campaign:spring). Company means the linked company partner, or the parent company when the linked partner is a person. One rule per tag; the weights add up with every other matching rule.

For a company metric, choose Company metric, a numeric operator, the metric slug (for example google-rating or google-review-count), and the number to compare. Leave Period blank to test the latest snapshot. Set Period to a number of days to test the change over that window (latest value minus the reading from the start of the window). The same company link is used: a person scores on the parent company's snapshots.
