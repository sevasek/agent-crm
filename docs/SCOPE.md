# Scope

What this CRM includes, and what it leaves out. The day-to-day version is [Using the CRM](USING.md).

Your services, offers, pipeline stages, fit rules, and task owners are settings you edit. They are not built into the software. A new install has a starter pipeline and nothing else. Sample services exist as an optional script. They do not load on their own.

When a deal enters a stage, the CRM runs only the automations you saved: a task, a follow-on deal, or both, and only for the service or offer you named. A new install has none, so changing stage does nothing extra.

## Included

- Contacts: companies and people, and a timeline of calls, notes, meetings, and research.
- Leads, and deals on a pipeline. You choose when a lead becomes a deal. You can mark a deal lost, give a reason, and merge duplicate leads.
- Labels on a deal, such as a campaign name.
- A next step and a date, with due and overdue marks, and a daily note when a follow-up is due.
- After a sale, another deal linked to the first, and tasks you hand to a person or an agent. Not invoices, and not a delivery tracker.
- A call list ranked by a fixed formula, and a call page meant for a phone.
- Light and dark colours that follow the device. There is no theme switch in the app.
- A connection for an AI agent. The tool list is in [MCP: the agent interface](MCP.md).
- Three optional notifications: nurture, a handed-off task, and a won deal. Leave the address blank and nothing is sent.

## Left out

- Email inside the CRM. Log a call or a note yourself, or let the agent log it.
- A phone app of its own. Use the site in the phone's browser.
- Several people with different permissions. One login is the design.
- Charts and dashboards.
- A built-in model that decides who to call. The scores are arithmetic you can read.
- Connections beyond those three notifications. Invoice status does not come back into the CRM.
